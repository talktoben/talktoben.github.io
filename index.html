About Ben

Fifteen years of knowing this coastline, one neighborhood at a time.

Ben Mirbagheri has spent the last fifteen years working Southern California's real estate market, from Orange County's beach towns to the hillsides of Los Angeles and the bluffs above San Diego. A UC San Diego graduate, he brings the same curiosity that sends him backpacking through new countries and chasing surf up and down the coast into every transaction — treating each client's search as its own project, not a script.

Whether you're buying your first place or selling a longtime home, Ben's approach is the same: know the market cold, communicate straight, and stay reachable.
